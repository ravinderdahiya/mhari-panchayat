Privacy Policy

Mhari Panchayat · Development and Panchayats Department, Haryana

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Mhari Panchayat ("the Service") is a Government of Haryana platform for Gram Panchayat governance. It lets citizens register complaints about village services and lets authorised officers — CPLO, Surveyor, Gram Sachiv, BDPO and DDPO — survey, verify and manage Panchayat assets and resolve complaints. The Service is operated by the Development and Panchayats Department, Haryana, with technical support from HARSAC (Haryana Space Applications Centre).

We collect only the information described in this policy and do not share it with anyone outside the relevant Government Administration.

The terms used here have the same meaning as in our Terms of Service, unless defined otherwise.

2. Information We Collect

Citizens. To register a complaint, we collect your mobile number (verified by OTP), your name, the village or ward the complaint relates to, the complaint description, and any photos you choose to attach. If you use "Use my location", your device location is recorded with the complaint so officers can find the site.

Officers and surveyors. Accounts are created by an administrator. For each account we hold your name, official email address, mobile number, designation, and assigned jurisdiction (district, block and village as per LGD codes). We also record login activity for security audit.

Survey and asset data. During field surveys, the app records asset details, photos, GPS coordinates and GPS accuracy, and the date and time of capture, along with the officer who captured, verified or approved the record.

The app asks for location, camera and storage permissions only when you use a feature that needs them, such as capturing an asset photo or tagging a complaint location. You can deny a permission, but the related feature may not work.

3. Offline Use and Storage on Your Device

Officers can capture survey data without network coverage. This data is stored temporarily in an encrypted local database on the device and is uploaded automatically once a connection is available. After a successful upload, the local copy is cleared on the next sync. Logging out removes locally stored data from the device.

4. Tracking and Cookies

The Service does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The web admin panel may use essential session cookies to keep you signed in. The app may use third-party libraries, such as mapping SDKs, that use similar technology to function. Declining them may limit features such as the map display.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • To register, route and track complaints to the responsible officer, and to escalate them when they are not resolved in time.
  • To maintain an accurate, verified register of Gram Panchayat assets.
  • To send you updates about the status of your complaint by SMS or in-app notification.
  • To keep an audit trail of who created, verified or changed a record.
  • To improve the Service and produce aggregated, non-identifying reports for planning.

6. Data Accuracy

Village, block and district boundaries and master data are based on the Local Government Directory (LGD) and records of the Government of Haryana. Information shown in the Service is for administrative and viewing purposes only and is not valid as evidence in any dispute in a court of law.

7. Sharing of Data

Data collected through the Service is used only by the Government. It may be shared between Haryana state government departments, such as the Development and Panchayats Department and HARSAC, and, where required, with the Union Government, for Panchayat administration and e-governance purposes. Complaint details are visible only to the officers responsible for resolving them.

8. Service Providers

We may engage third parties — developers, hosting providers, SMS gateways and mapping service providers — to:

  • Facilitate or provide the Service on our behalf;
  • Perform Service-related functions; or
  • Help us analyse how the Service is used.

These parties act only under Government direction and may not use your data for any other purpose.

9. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on servers located in India. We use measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, account lockout after repeated failed logins, role-based access, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

Complaint and asset records are kept as long as required for Panchayat administration and as per applicable government record-retention rules. Officer accounts are deactivated when an officer leaves the assigned role.

11. Links to Other Sites

The Service may link to other government or department portals. These sites are not operated by us, and we are not responsible for their content or practices. Please review their privacy policies.

12. Your Rights

You may ask us to correct details in a complaint you submitted. The Government retains the right to correct or delete data collected through the Service.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect as soon as they are posted on this page. Please review it periodically.

14. Contact Us

For questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@harsac.org.in.
